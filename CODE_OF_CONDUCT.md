# Code of Conduct

2dbench compares other people's work in public. That only works if every library's maintainers and users are treated with respect, even when the numbers aren't kind to their library.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent. Every library here is someone's long-term work.
- Discuss results, adapters and methods, not people or communities.
- Back claims with measurements, and accept measurements that contradict you.
- Make room for newcomers, and help them find their footing.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Using results here to mock or attack a library, its authors or its users.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in every 2dbench space: the repository, issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere someone represents the project. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and comments, and may block people who break these rules.

To report a problem, open a GitHub issue (or, for anything sensitive, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub). Reports are handled confidentially.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
