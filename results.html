2dbench community results

Every result in the 2dbench repository, one row per device. Desktop rows passed the pixel-parity gate; phone rows come from mobile.html. Compare libraries within a row: absolute numbers depend on the device, the day and its temperature.

Is your device missing? Phones, tablets, Chromebooks, older laptops, Safari and Firefox are all wanted. Open mobile.html on it, tap Run, then Submit on GitHub. It takes a few minutes, and your results land here once merged.
